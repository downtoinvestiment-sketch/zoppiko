# Consistency checklist (every storyboard and every final video)

Official references: `references/zop_turnaround.jpg`, `references/piko_turnaround.jpg`, `references/zop_expressions.jpg`,
`references/piko_expressions.jpg`, `references/zop_piko_interaction.jpg`. Locked values: `CHARACTER_SPEC.json`.

## Design (must match the references)

- [ ] Zop: orange head and limbs, one orange curled antenna, turquoise shirt, dark blue shorts, cream shoes with teal soles.
- [ ] Piko: blue head and limbs, one blue curled antenna, yellow shirt, dark slate shorts, white shoes with coral soles.
- [ ] Same proportions as the turnaround (about 2.8 heads tall), same eyes, nose, mouth; no extra parts, no costumes.
- [ ] Only the engine variants `zop` and `piko` are used; no material or colour overrides on the characters.

## Staging

- [ ] Both faces readable in every shot that carries a reaction; nobody turns his back on the joke.
- [ ] No intersections: heads, bodies, hands and props never pass through each other.
- [ ] Faces and key action between 250 and 1550 px from the top, left of 940 px in the lower half (app buttons).
- [ ] The antenna matches the emotion: "?" calm or curious, "!" surprise or joy, drooping when sad or dizzy.

## Comedy and sound

- [ ] Something moves in the first frame; the goal is clear within 2 seconds.
- [ ] Rule of three or a clear twist; a held reaction (12–18 frames); a last beat that loops.
- [ ] Every impact, press and fall has its sound, in sync to the frame; one real silence before the payoff.
- [ ] No words, no text on screen, no logo; music credited in the description when it is CC-BY.
